A fan-made Chainsaw Man mod.

- Pochita's Heart: become a Devil Hybrid. Melee hits drink blood, and Pochita saves you from death once every few minutes.
- Starter Cord: pull it to turn into the Chainsaw Devil for 30 seconds.
- Chainsaw Arm: a chainsaw weapon that cuts trees and enemies.
- Pochita Doll: summons Pochita as a pet.

This is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with Tatsuki Fujimoto, Shueisha or MAPPA.
