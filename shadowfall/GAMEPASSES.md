# Game pass store descriptions

Copy these into each game pass's **Description** on the Creator Dashboard. They're kept short so they read well on the store page. Every pass can also be earned for free in game with Tokens, and the descriptions say so.

---

### Power Vault
Never lose a power again! Every power you own or roll is saved in your Vault forever, even if you replace it. Swap between your saved powers any time from the Power menu. Also unlockable for free in game with Tokens.

### Lucky Star
Fortune favours you. Every power roll is 1.5x more likely to land Legendary or better, forever. Stacks with Star Shards for even better odds. Also unlockable for free in game with Tokens.

### Second Wind
Refuse to fall. Once every 5 minutes, a hit that would knock you out leaves you standing at 30% health, with a moment of protection to get back in the fight. Also unlockable for free in game with Tokens.

### Fleet Feet
Outrun the dark. Walk and sprint 12% faster in the shadow world, so you reach bosses, townsfolk and your base sooner. Also unlockable for free in game with Tokens.

### Builder's Belt
Build your base faster. Get 50% more Wood, Stone, Scrap and every other material from trees, rocks and wrecks on average. Also unlockable for free in game with Tokens.

### Steady Mind
Keep your head when the lights go out. Your sanity drains 35% slower everywhere, so the shadows' whispers take longer to get to you. Stacks with the Lantern. Also unlockable for free in game with Tokens.

### Biscuit's Glow
Your call duck shines brighter! Biscuit's comforting aura reaches 50% further and restores your sanity and health 50% faster. Quack. Also unlockable for free in game with Tokens.

### Radiant Aura
Stand out in the shadows. A glow in your power's colour lights the ground around you, and other players see you sparkle. Cosmetic only, and you can switch it on or off any time. Also unlockable for free in game with Tokens.

### Biscuit's Wardrobe
Dress up Biscuit! Unlock a bow tie, a top hat, a flower crown and a cosy scarf for your call duck, and swap outfits any time. Cosmetic only (but very cute). Also unlockable for free in game with Tokens.
