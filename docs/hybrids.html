Hybrid forms

Seven more devil hybrids, each with its own transform item, perk and abilities. All of them are crafted from Devil Flesh at an Anvil, so you can use them from very early on.

  • Reze

How they work

Use a form's item to transform and use it again to turn back. There's no time limit and no cooldown. Transforming into a hybrid ends any other form, Chainsaw Man included. Unlike Chainsaw Man, each hybrid has a single form that grows stronger as you defeat bosses. Every form also makes you immune to knockback and fall damage, and gives a perk of its own.

Each row is one more of those five bosses beaten, in any order. The damage bonus applies to all damage types. Ability items work the same way as Chainsaw Man's: they appear in empty inventory slots while you're transformed and vanish when you turn back. Their base damage also gains the same progression bonus as the Chainsaw Arm.

Reze and Katana Man use real sprite sheets. The other five forms still use simple placeholder sprites that are ready to be replaced.
