Devils & bosses

Seven devil enemies roam the world and drop the Devil Flesh that most of the mod is crafted from. Five bosses, each summoned with its own item, take you from pre-Hardmode all the way past the Moon Lord.

Zombie Devil

Pre-Hardmode boss · 3,500 life · 30 damage · 12 defense

Summon: Rotting Offering (8 Devil Flesh @ Demon/Crimson Altar).

A hulking devil that commands an army of the dead. It walks at you, leaps and slams the ground to spray blood bolts, and raises Zombie Devil Minions. It gets faster below 50% life.

Drops: 15-25 Devil Flesh, Chainsaw Arm (33%), Pochita's Heart (25%).

Bat Devil

Hardmode boss · 28,000 life · 60 damage · 30 defense

Summon: Bloody Bat Fang (15 Devil Flesh, 5 Soul of Night @ Mythril/Orichalcum Anvil). Only works in Hardmode.

A giant devil that drinks blood to grow stronger. It flies above you firing spreads of blood bolts, then winds up and dashes. Below 50% life it fires wider spreads, dashes twice and calls swarms of Devil Bats.

Drops: 20-30 Devil Flesh, 15-25 Soul of Flight, Revved Starter Cord (25%).

Eternity Devil

Hardmode boss · 40,000 life · 70 damage · 35 defense

Summon: Cursed Hotel Key (20 Devil Flesh, 8 Soul of Night, Golden Key @ Mythril/Orichalcum Anvil). Only works in Hardmode.

A giant drifting mass of flesh that fires rings of blood and spits out Eternity Fleshlings.

It regenerates while any Fleshlings are alive, so kill them first.

Drops: 25-40 Devil Flesh, 10-20 Soul of Night, 10-20 Soul of Light.

Gun Devil

Post-Plantera boss · 55,000 life · 90 damage · 45 defense

Summon: Gun Devil's Trigger (15 Gun Devil Fragment, 10 Devil Flesh @ Mythril/Orichalcum Anvil). Only works after Plantera has been defeated.

One of the most feared devils. It circles you with rapid gunfire, stops to fire a bullet spiral, then charges three times. Below 50% life it's faster and calls Gun Devil Spawn.

Drops: 20-35 Gun Devil Fragment, 30-50 Devil Flesh, 15-25 Chlorophyte Bar.

Makima

Post-Moon Lord boss · 150,000 life · 120 damage · 60 defense

Summon: Makima's Contract (10 Luminite Bar, 10 Gun Devil Fragment, 20 Devil Flesh @ Ancient Manipulator). Only works after the Moon Lord has been defeated.

She walks toward you and cycles through her attacks:

  • Bang: finger-gun shots.
  • Hounds: white hounds burst from the ground under you. Watch for bubbling blood.
  • Gun fiends: floating gun fiends that spray bullets.
  • Contract: calls other devils to fight for her.
  • Teleport: she melts into blood and reappears.
  • Kick combos: if you get close, she lunges in with one of two kick combos.

She takes half damage while any of her devils are alive. At half life she's knocked down, gets back up, and everything gets faster.

Drops: Hero of Hell's Cord (33%), 20-30 Luminite Bar, 40-60 Devil Flesh, 20-30 Gun Devil Fragment.
