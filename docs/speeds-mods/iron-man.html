Marvel: Iron Man

Become Iron Man and design your own suit. Power it with an Arc Reactor, fly, fire Repulsors and the Unibeam, customise every part in the Suit Workshop, buy premium sets and weapons in the Parts Store, and turn giant.

Getting started

  1. Craft the basics

    A Stark Tablet and an Arc Reactor Mk I (recipes below).

  2. Equip the Arc Reactor

    Wear it as an accessory. Only one Arc Reactor can be worn at a time.

  3. Suit up

    Press I or right-click with the Stark Tablet. Press it again to suit down.

  4. Design your suit

    Press O or left-click with the Stark Tablet to open the Suit Workshop.

  5. Shop for parts

    Press P to open the Parts Store and buy more parts with coins.

  6. Go giant

    Once you've bought the Giant Suit, press G while suited up.

All four keys can be rebound in Settings → Controls → Mod Controls.

Every category is independent, so the options combine into about 5.2 × 1050 different suits. You start with the classic red-and-gold parts; other parts are bought in the Parts Store. Colours, Glow Strength and Glow Pulse are always free.

The Systems options change how the suit plays. For example, Repulsor Mode can be Rapid, Spread, Heavy, Piercing or Twin, and Hover thrusters hold your altitude when you let go. The workshop also has a live animated preview and Random Armour, Random Colours and Random Everything buttons.

Install

  1. Copy the repository into your ModSources folder as MarvelMod. Rename it if you cloned it as marvel-mod.
  2. In tModLoader open Workshop → Develop Mods and click Build + Reload next to Marvel: Iron Man.
