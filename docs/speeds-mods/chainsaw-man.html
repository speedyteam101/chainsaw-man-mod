Chainsaw Man

Pull the cord and become the Chainsaw Devil. Pochita, three tiers of Chainsaw Man transformation, seven devil hybrid forms, a chainsaw weapon, and a long list of devils and bosses ending with Makima after the Moon Lord.

This mod has its own, much more detailed wiki with sprites, stats and recipes for everything: open the Chainsaw Man Mod Wiki.

Features

Pochita's Heart

Become a Devil Hybrid. Melee hits drink blood, and Pochita saves you from death once every few minutes.

Starter Cord

Turn into the Chainsaw Devil and back, with no time limit or cooldown. Upgrade it in Hardmode and after the Moon Lord for stronger forms.

Chainsaw Arm

A held chainsaw that cuts through trees and enemies, and hits much harder while you're transformed.

Hybrid forms

Reze (Bomb), Katana Man, Quanxi (Bow), Barem Bridge (Flamethrower), Miri Sugo (Sword), the Spear Hybrid and the Whip Hybrid.

Devils & bosses

Zombie Devil minions, devil bats, Gun Devil Spawn and many more, plus bosses including the Four Horsemen War, Famine and Death, the Darkness Devil and Makima.

Items & recipes

Every craftable item, material and summon item.

Progression in short

  1. Pre-boss

    Craft Pochita's Heart (Life Crystal, 5 Chain, Shackle @ Demon/Crimson Altar) and the Starter Cord (10 Rope, 3 Iron/Lead Bar @ Anvil), then transform.

  2. Hardmode

    Upgrade to the Revved Starter Cord with Hallowed Bars and the three mechanical boss souls.

  3. Post-Moon Lord

    Craft the Hero of Hell's Cord for the strongest form, then face Makima.

Install

Clone the repository into your ModSources folder as ChainsawManMod (the folder name must match the mod name), then use Workshop → Develop Mods → Build + Reload. See the install page for a fix for the most common error.
